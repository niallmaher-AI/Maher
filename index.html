About the Chillout Room

This app is for the Maher family to book the Chillout Room fairly. Tom and Olivia earn credit minutes for chores and good behaviour. They can spend those minutes on gaming, reading, music, chilling — or save up for a sleepover.
Parents are admins. They add credit, approve sleepovers, and can clear or cancel any booking.
